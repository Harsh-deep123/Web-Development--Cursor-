After going through feedback about the Digital Lab Series over the last month, I kept hearing the same things:

“I know they could benefit from it, but I couldn’t get them to do it.”

“The content is great, but they didn’t stay engaged the whole time.”

“We’d still like them to meet with you, even if it’s only once a month.”

I definitely understand… Between school, sports, siblings, activities and everything else, getting to regular sessions can be tough. Getting your kid to sit down and do another “thing” on their own can be even tougher.

So, I created T.E.A.M.S. Flex, a hybrid option that combines the personal connection of 1-on-1 coaching with the Digital Lab Series in a way that is guided, personalized and manageable.

I’m reaching out to your family first because of our positive experience working together and my understanding of your kid and your family’s needs.

**T.E.A.M.S. Flex — $199/month**
- One monthly 1-on-1 session with me, virtual or in-person
- Brief virtual check-in between sessions
- Full access to the Digital Lab Series
- Personalized Lab content based on what your kid is actually dealing with

- Student input on what they want to work on
- Goals, action steps and accountability

That means parents can stay involved without having to convince their kid to do it or manage the process at home.

The goal is simple: keep the personal connection, use the right resources at the right time, and make T.E.A.M.S. work better for busy families.

I’m excited to offer this and would love to have your family be part of it. If you’re interested, just reply and we can get started!
